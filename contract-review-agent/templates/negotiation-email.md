# Negotiation Email

Drafted after the risk table. Send-ready tone: collaborative, specific, under 200 words.

```text
Subject: {{contract_name}} — a few small changes before we sign

Hi {{client_first_name}},

Thanks for sending this over — happy to get started. Before signing, I'd like to adjust three things so the agreement matches how we both work:

1. {{ask_one_theme}}: I'd suggest {{ask_one_language}}.
2. {{ask_two_theme}}: {{ask_two_language}}.
3. {{ask_three_theme}}: {{ask_three_language}}.

Everything else looks good to me, and I'm ready to sign as soon as these are reflected.

If any of these are tricky on your side, tell me which and I'll propose an alternative — I'm sure we can find something that works.

Best,
{{sender_name}}
```

Rules:

- Three asks maximum. More than three reads as a rewrite, not a negotiation.
- Lead with the ask, then the proposed language. Never explain at length why the client is being unfair.
- Group by theme, not by section number, unless the client's legal team needs the reference.
- Offer one fallback in the closing line; it keeps the exchange collaborative.
- No threats, no deadlines unless the user explicitly set one, no mention of lawyers.
- Only include asks the user confirmed. The review may list eight findings; the email carries the three that matter.
- Placeholders are for the user to fill; do not send the email yourself.

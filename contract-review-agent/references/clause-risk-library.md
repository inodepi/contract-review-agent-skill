# Clause Risk Library

Patterns that cost freelancers and small agencies money, with the red-flag phrases to search for and the default ask. Severity is a starting point — adjust by contract value and client power.

## Payment terms

**Red flags:** `net-90`, `pay-when-paid`, `upon client's receipt of funds from its customer`, `at client's sole discretion`, invoicing only after final acceptance, no deposit, no late fee.

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Net-60 or longer | High | Net-30 from invoice date; deposit 30–50% |
| Pay-when-paid / pay-if-paid | Critical | Remove; payment due on invoice date regardless of client's collections |
| Payment tied to end-customer acceptance | High | Acceptance criteria and a deemed-accepted date (e.g. 10 business days) |
| No deposit on new client | Medium | 30–50% upfront or milestone-based |
| No late fee | Medium | 1.5%/month or statutory maximum, whichever is lower |
| Unlimited revisions | High | Two rounds included; further rounds at an hourly rate |
| Expenses unaddressed | Low | Pre-approved, at cost, itemised |

## Intellectual property

**Red flags:** `all right, title and interest`, `work made for hire`, `including all pre-existing materials`, `moral rights`, `perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide`.

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assignment of pre-existing tools/templates | Critical | License pre-existing IP to client; assign only bespoke deliverables on full payment |
| No portfolio/marketing rights | Medium | Right to display work after publication, with credit |
| IP transfers before payment | High | Transfer on receipt of full payment |
| Broad "moral rights" waiver | Low | Accept for deliverables; do not waive for pre-existing work |
| Client owns concepts and pitch materials | Medium | Limit to final deliverables; retain concepts |

## Liability and indemnity

**Red flags:** `unlimited liability`, `including consequential, indirect and punitive damages`, `indemnify, defend and hold harmless`, `any and all claims`.

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No liability cap | Critical | Cap at fees paid (or 12 months of fees); exclude consequential damages |
| Unlimited indemnity | Critical | Mutual indemnity limited to the indemnifying party's negligence or IP infringement |
| One-way indemnity | High | Make mutual; cap it |
| Client can claim lost profits | High | Exclude indirect and consequential losses |
| Insurance requirement unstated | Medium | Confirm limits match the contract; do not promise coverage you do not have |

## Termination

**Red flags:** `terminate for convenience without notice`, `no compensation for work in progress`, `sole discretion`, `immediately upon notice`.

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Termination for convenience, no kill fee | High | 30 days' notice plus payment for work performed and committed costs |
| Asymmetric notice | Medium | Same notice both ways |
| Deliverables withheld until payment in full | Medium | Acceptable if the client is current on invoices |
| Termination on insolvency only | Low | Fine; check payment protection elsewhere |

## Restrictive covenants

**Red flags:** `shall not provide services to any competitor`, `for a period of twelve months`, `worldwide`, `any business similar to`.

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Non-compete wider than the engagement | High | Limit to the client's direct competitors, the actual services provided, 6 months |
| Broad non-solicit of all client contacts | Medium | Limit to contacts introduced during the engagement |
| Exclusivity with no minimum commitment | High | Exclusivity only against a minimum monthly fee |
| Worldwide geographic scope | Medium | Limit to markets where the client operates |

## Auto-renewal

**Red flags:** `automatically renew`, `unless either party provides written notice no later than ninety (90) days`.

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evergreen renewal with long notice window | Medium | 30-day notice, or renewal only on written agreement |
| Price locked with no increase mechanism | Low | Annual CPI or negotiated uplift |
| Renewal term longer than the original | Medium | Match original term or monthly thereafter |

## Scope and change control

**Red flags:** `as needed`, `all related services`, `client may request additional work`, `deemed accepted`.

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vague deliverables ("website redesign") | High | Written deliverable list with acceptance criteria |
| No change-order process | High | Changes outside the SOW priced and approved in writing |
| Deemed acceptance with no review window | Medium | 10 business days to review; silence = acceptance is acceptable |
| Open-ended support obligations | High | Defined hours, response times, and a monthly cap |

## Confidentiality

**Red flags:** `perpetual`, `any information disclosed`, `including the existence of this agreement`.

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-way confidentiality | Medium | Mutual |
| Indefinite duration | Low | 3–5 years post-termination, trade secrets as long as required |
| Definition covers everything | Medium | Limit to marked or reasonably confidential information |
| No carve-out for prior knowledge or public info | Low | Standard carve-outs |

## Boilerplate

| Finding | Risk | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foreign governing law or venue | Medium | Your jurisdiction, or neutral arbitration |
| Client may assign to any successor | Medium | Consent required; no assignment to a direct competitor |
| Contractor cannot assign or subcontract | Low | Allow subcontractors under your responsibility |
| No warranty disclaimer | Medium | Services provided "as is"; no guarantee of results |
| Unilateral amendment rights | High | Amendments only in writing signed by both parties |

## Missing clauses that are findings

- liability cap and exclusion of consequential damages
- late-payment interest
- kill fee on termination for convenience
- change-order process
- portfolio rights
- acceptance criteria and deemed-acceptance date
- payment on termination
- ownership transfer conditioned on payment
- dispute resolution / escalation path

An absent clause is not automatically severe — but if its absence leaves the user exposed to a loss larger than the contract, it is Critical.
